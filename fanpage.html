THE WEEKND

Singer · Songwriter · Producer
Abel Makkonen Tesfaye

Biography

The Weeknd is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and record producer known for his distinctive falsetto, genre-blending sound, and cinematic visual style. Rising from an underground mixtape scene in Toronto in the early 2010s, he built a devoted following before crossing into mainstream pop stardom with albums that fused R&B, synth-pop, and dark, atmospheric production. Over the years he has become one of the best-selling and most streamed artists in the world, known for chart-topping singles, sold-out stadium tours, and a signature aesthetic built around his "XO" brand.
